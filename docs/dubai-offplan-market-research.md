> Generated 2026-06-08 via a multi-agent deep-research run: 105 agents, 23 sources fetched, 95 claims extracted, 25 adversarially verified (3-vote), 4 refuted and excluded.

## 3. Developers

- **Verified ranking:** Emaar led 2025 sales (~AED 51bn), followed by DAMAC (~AED 24bn). *(Medium, 2-1.)*
- Major active off-plan developers: Emaar, DAMAC, Nakheel, Sobha, Meraas, Azizi, Danube, Binghatti, Ellington, Select Group, Omniyat, MAG, Deyaar, Samana, Object 1, Reportage, Imtiaz, Beyond.
- **Excluded (refuted):** Blog-sourced Q1-2026 developer league tables (e.g. "DAMAC led at 4,049 units sold") failed verification 0-3. **Do not quote these to investors.**

## 4. Communities / areas

- Other major off-plan communities: Dubai Hills Estate, Dubai Creek Harbour, MBR City, Dubai Marina, Downtown, Dubailand, DAMAC Hills, Emaar South, Expo City, Sobha Hartland, Maritime City.

## 6. Market structure

- **Escrow:** Dubai **Law No. 8 of 2007** mandates per-project escrow accounts, ring-fenced from developer creditors (Articles 3 & 9). Verified 3-0.
- **Oqood:** Off-plan purchase registration prerequisite.
